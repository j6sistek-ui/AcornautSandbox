About the studio

Quarterdrop LLC

Independent game studio · Novi, Michigan, United States

Quarterdrop LLC is a small independent studio that makes arcade games for phones and the web.

Its first game is Acornaut, a one-touch arcade flight game. You can play it free, right now, at acornaut.app.

Acornaut

Tap to fly, thread the gates, and see how far you can get. Acornaut runs in any modern browser, and versions for the Apple App Store and Google Play are on the way.
