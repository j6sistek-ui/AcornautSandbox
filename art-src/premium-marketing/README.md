# Premium Shop artwork

Every catalog bundle has its own marketing banner, created with the built-in
image generator using the actual included game assets as identity references.
These banners are exclusive to bundle offers. Individual product cards and
contents previews use the shipped game art and painters, including all three
premium pilots. Their flight sheets and registration are unchanged.

| Master | Shipping file under `docs/art/shop/` | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bundles/<bundle-id>.png` (7 masters) | `<bundle-id>.png` | 1024 × 512 |
| `trio.png` | `bundle-premium-trio.png` | 1024 × 512 |
| `stardust-emblem.png` | `stardust-emblem.png` | 256 × 256 |
| `shop-nebula.png` | `shop-nebula.png` | 1200 × 800 |

The four retained group briefs and exact game-art reference paths are in
[bundle-prompts.json](bundle-prompts.json); three new three-product companion
and visor groups are in [group-prompts.json](group-prompts.json).
Single-item and two-item banner drafts are excluded from shipping and this PR.
The Aurora and Regalia calls use
the supported five-reference limit as recorded in
[bundle-retries.json](bundle-retries.json). Visual review required a targeted
Leviathan closed-visor correction in Regalia;
[regalia-repair-prompt.json](regalia-repair-prompt.json) records that edit.
The previously approved trio, crystal emblem and background briefs are in
[additional-prompts.json](additional-prompts.json). Three earlier portrait
studies (`porcelain.png`, `nacre.png`, `origamist.png`) and their
[briefs](prompts.json) remain only as source references for the trio; they
are not exported or used on individual item cards.

Run `node illustrated-src/export-shop-art.mjs` from the repository root to
resize the approved masters after compiling the current catalog. The standard
sandbox exporter runs it in that order. It requires every kit's unique banner
and valid editable discount, rejects a changed aspect ratio, and records SHA-256,
dimensions and byte sizes in [export-receipt.json](export-receipt.json).
There are ten shipping images: eight real bundle banners and two Shop graphics.
Each bundle contains at least three distinct products. No runtime suit, helmet, companion,
trail, gameplay sheet or anchor is generated by this exporter.

Prices, savings and labels are live HTML, never text baked into artwork.
Selecting a bundle opens every typed included item plus deduplicated bonus
wakes, then its actual game preview. Built-in wakes are shown on their owning
suit. The [kit pricing guide](../../illustrated-src/design/shop-refresh/PRICING.md)
documents all eight prices, editing a discount, full individual ownership credit,
and explicit zero-cost completion. The same rule is required in `AGENTS.md`.

See [Shop QA](../../illustrated-src/design/shop-refresh/QAplan.md) for the
baseline and verification criteria. Earlier screenshots are explicitly marked
superseded; use the final verification receipt and browser captures for review.
