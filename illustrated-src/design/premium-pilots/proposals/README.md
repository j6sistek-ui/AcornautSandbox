# Additional premium pilot proposals

These five cards preserve the existing 9 September 2026 concept artwork for review. They are **proposal-only**: no new artwork, production kits, rigging, runtime assets, catalog entries or purchase configuration are supplied for these characters.

[Open the local card gallery](index.html). All four PNG files are byte-identical copies of the original boards; [provenance.json](provenance.json) records their SHA-256 hashes. No crops, edits or new image generation were made for this proposal package.

## Scope

The owner selected **Porcelain Paragon with Sovereign Shell (helmet B), always worn; Nacre Envoy, helmetless by design; and Foldspace Origamist with Facet Shell, always worn** for production elsewhere in this change. They are not additional proposals here. The shared bonus board retains Origamist as original context only; its illustrated bare head and Survey Dome are not its selected production configuration.

The material and motion descriptions below paraphrase the original concept review. Helmet names follow the existing boards. All suggested construction and behavior remain design ideas, without pricing or implementation commitments.

## Nocturne Atelier

[Existing concept board](02-nocturne-atelier.png)

- **Character:** black squirrel with silver cheek flecks.
- **Materials:** black-cherry technical satin, oxblood glass and narrow rose-titanium frames. Warm burgundy distinguishes the tailored fabric/metal treatment.
- **Helmet studies:** Salon Dome, Nightglass and Opera Frame.
- **Possible motion:** measured movement with soft tail follow-through and restrained burgundy light ribbons.
- **Unresolved production work:** preserve dark-fur readability at flight size and against dark zones; fit the paintings and helmet openings to the common anatomy.

## Calibre Meridian

[Existing concept board](03-calibre-meridian.png)

- **Character:** russet squirrel with cream facial markings.
- **Materials:** espresso vitreous enamel, brushed platinum, sapphire windows and an inset escapement. Precision instrument craft is the intended identity.
- **Helmet studies:** Observatory, Regulator and Meridian.
- **Possible motion:** finely damped movement, a close-up chest mechanism and short timing arcs in the wake.
- **Unresolved production work:** contain any mechanism animation inside its panel; maintain clear separation from existing clockwork-themed cosmetics and fit the shared rig.

## Wayfarer Atlas

[Existing concept board](04-wayfarer-atlas.png)

- **Character:** red panda with white facial patches, rust cheeks, dark lower limbs and a ringed tail.
- **Materials:** cream and burnt-orange expedition armor, titanium hardware and smoked-amber navigation glass.
- **Helmet studies:** Survey Dome, Horizon Shell and Chartglass.
- **Possible motion:** confident forward reach, weightier tail recovery and an amber contour-map wake.
- **Unresolved production work:** newly measured art and helmet fitting; preserve red-panda facial identity and tail markings at gameplay size.

## Velvet Navigator

[Existing shared bonus board, center column](06-bonus-explorations.png)

- **Character:** moth pilot with oatmeal/sienna fluff, amber eyes, antennae and patterned wings.
- **Materials:** soft fur and plum expedition fabric, with warm metal fittings.
- **Helmet studies:** Survey Globe and Orbit Helmet.
- **Possible motion:** a wing-based silhouette with antennae kept within a controlled head envelope.
- **Unresolved production work:** paired wings require an explicit rig design; the existing single-tail topology does not establish readiness for this character.

## Rivet & Ribbon

[Existing shared bonus board, right column](06-bonus-explorations.png)

- **Character:** collectible tin-toy squirrel with a ribbon-spring tail.
- **Materials:** ivory enamel, cherry-red joints, visible rivets and small winding-key details.
- **Helmet studies:** Clockwork Dome and Pressure Helmet.
- **Possible motion:** toy-like stepped recovery and a controlled spring-tail response.
- **Unresolved production work:** prototype the spring tail and limit protrusions; distinguish its silhouette and movement from the existing Robo.

## Review boundary

The illustrations are presentation concepts, including speculative flight views. They are not screenshots, cut-part masters, fitted helmets or evidence of runtime behavior. Their inclusion in this PR preserves options for a later owner decision and adds no playable or purchasable characters.
