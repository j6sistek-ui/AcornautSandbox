Five directions for a future flight

Existing premium pilot concepts, preserved for review. Each card is proposal-only. The artwork below is unchanged from the original 9 September 2026 boards; no new images or production assets were generated for these five characters.

Selected production scope: Porcelain Paragon with Sovereign Shell (B), always worn; Nacre Envoy, helmetless by design; Foldspace Origamist with Facet Shell, always worn. Origamist appears only as context in the unchanged shared bonus board below.

Nocturne Atelier

Black squirrel · tailored luxury

Material identity
Black-cherry technical satin, oxblood glass and rose-titanium frames; dark fur with silver cheek flecks.
Helmet studies
Salon Dome · Nightglass · Opera Frame
Motion idea
Measured motion, soft tail follow-through and restrained burgundy light ribbons.

Still to explore: dark-zone and flight-size readability, common anatomy fitting and helmet clearances.

Proposal only · existing artwork

Calibre Meridian

Russet squirrel · precision instrument craft

Material identity
Espresso vitreous enamel, brushed platinum, sapphire windows and an inset escapement.
Helmet studies
Observatory · Regulator · Meridian
Motion idea
Finely damped motion, a close-up chest mechanism and short timing arcs in the wake.

Still to explore: a contained mechanism treatment, fit to the shared rig and distinction from existing clockwork cosmetics.

Proposal only · existing artwork

Wayfarer Atlas

Red panda · expedition equipment

Material identity
Cream and burnt-orange armor, titanium hardware and smoked-amber navigation glass; distinctive face patches and a plush ringed tail.
Helmet studies
Survey Dome · Horizon Shell · Chartglass
Motion idea
Confident forward reach, weightier tail recovery and a subtle amber contour-map wake.

Still to explore: newly measured art and helmet fitting, with recognizable red-panda markings at gameplay size.

Proposal only · existing artwork

Velvet Navigator

Moth pilot · soft explorer

View the center column of the shared bonus board or open the original image.

Material identity
Oatmeal/sienna fluff, amber eyes, plum expedition fabric, warm metal fittings and patterned wings.
Helmet studies
Survey Globe · Orbit Helmet
Motion idea
A wing-based silhouette, with antennae kept within a controlled head envelope.

Still to explore: paired wings require an explicit rig design. The existing single-tail topology does not establish production readiness.

Proposal only · existing artwork

Rivet & Ribbon

Tin-toy squirrel · collectible charm

View the right column of the shared bonus board or open the original image.

Material identity
Ivory enamel, cherry-red joints, visible rivets, winding-key details and a ribbon-spring tail.
Helmet studies
Clockwork Dome · Pressure Helmet
Motion idea
Toy-like stepped recovery and a controlled spring-tail response.

Still to explore: spring-tail behavior and silhouette limits, with a clear distinction from the existing Robo.
