# Concept review notes

Seven final boards cover all 26 zones. All use the built-in image tool. Initial prompts are in `image-prompts.json`; two focused debris-count corrections are recorded in `image-edit-prompts.json`. Concept art was visually inspected after those corrections. Every final row depicts five planets; debris counts follow the complete two/three-item roster. The art is a proposal, not a set of accepted shipping sprites.

The board studies reinterpret existing assets. The numbered original thumbnails and `ASSET_OWNERSHIP.md` identify what is actually retained unchanged. In particular, the illustrated Canopy World, Rose Reef, Night Market and several debris pieces are not pixel-identical previews of the source assets. Do not cut those cells out to replace retained originals.

Further art-direction refinements before production:

- Golden Hour: the amber and honeycomb studies read a little molten. Remove the lava-like glow from those two surfaces and make the honeycomb broad, non-emissive cellular plates. The written amber/bronze direction controls.
- Solar Furnace: Magma Heart and Vent World need greater distinction in their final 256px art; favor three large vent basins on Vent World rather than another crack network.
- Monochrome Void versus Ghost Nebula: keep neutral, hard-edged geology for Monochrome; give Ghost eroded, smoky, weathered surfaces and clear dark boundaries. Their pale and dark planets should not become simple recolors of the same moon.
- Acid Swamp: Petrol Marsh still resembles a colorful continental world in the board. Its final surface should use dark oily pools rather than bright rainbow landmasses, to distinguish it from Hypervivid.
- Time Fracture: give Two Ages an architectural/aged-alloy half with no ordinary fossil silhouette as the dominant landmark; Bone Desert owns the fossil-led look.
- Event Horizon: Lens Scar must remain a solid oval basin. Deepen its solid center rather than accidentally turning it into another open vortex.
- Coral/Crystal: any outcrops outside the circular core need a measured collision/readability review. Ghost/Blackout/void debris require especially careful comparison against the actual bright/dark background patches.

These are bounded concept limitations, not implemented defects. Individual production masters and transparent cutouts are not part of this proposal PR.
