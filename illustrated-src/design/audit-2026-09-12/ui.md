Read-only. Rendered with Playwright/Chromium 141 against `python3 -m http.server 8765 --directory docs`, seeded save (`guide:"done"`, 1,234 acorns, 560 dust, 6 stars, `introOff`), production (`/index.html`) and beta (`/beta/index.html`). Viewports: 390x844, 375x667, 430x932 (dpr 2, touch) plus 844x390 landscape, a simulated notch (CDP `Emulation.setSafeAreaInsetsOverride` top 47 / bottom 34 — `env()` verified to read 47px), `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `prefers-color-scheme: light`, and a first-login daily toast. Every defect below is backed by a PNG in this directory or a `getBoundingClientRect`/computed-style measurement recorded in `*_metrics.json`. Nothing under the repo was modified.

Scratch dir: the audit session scratch (not tracked) (367 PNGs, 12 metrics JSONs, `drive.cjs` driver, `hdr.cjs`, `wallet.cjs`, `pause2.cjs`, `sheet.py`).
