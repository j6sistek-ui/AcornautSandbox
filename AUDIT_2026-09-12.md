## 3. A new player's first minute

The owner's report: new users say the game is glitchy and laggy, likely while it loads, and delete it before trying. Measured here with a fresh profile (no save, cold cache) at 390x844 on production, once at phone speed (4 Mbps, 150 ms round trip, CPU 4x slower) and once unthrottled, recording every request, every image decode with its size, long tasks, frame times and a CPU profile of the first flight. The full study is `illustrated-src/design/audit-2026-09-12/loading.md`.

One caveat: this container has no GPU, so canvas raster is software and the absolute frame times here are worse than a phone's. The loader work (decode, sprite measuring, the sort) is plain main-thread JavaScript and transfers as measured; the sim and painter were ruled out (§3.2, item 9).

### 3.1 What the player sees at phone speed

|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| 10 s | acorn loader still cycling (it is a barber pole, not progress) | 107 | 4.0 | 13 | 3 |
| **17.8 s** | **"TAP TO START" appears because the 12 s cap fires (`standalone.ts:158-161`), not because the art is home**: 51% of the boot art is still in flight | 175 | 9.0 | 38 | 9 |
| 25.7 s | LAUNCH tapped: a 934 ms task (course build, procedural sky, the 1.8 MB zone scene, the flight track) | 250 | 13.4 | 48 | 17 |
| 27-33 s | first flight, 267-300 ms per frame; every hitch window contains 3-9 boot images being measured on the main thread | 278 | 14.1 | 54 | 24 |
| 33-47 s | still the first flight; the idle sweep has started under it (pal and suit banks) | 348 | 17.6 | 85 | 59 |

Unthrottled, the title comes at 3.2 s and the sweep settles at 37 s at **1,352 requests, 78 MB transferred, 397 MB decoded and resident**. SHIPPING.md §4 says 220 requests and 13.5 MB; that was measured at stamp 222 and the catalog has since grown to 20 pal banks and 33 lazy suit banks. Even unthrottled, the first flight hitches 13 times over 100 ms in 20 s, one hitch per pal bank landing (13-36 decodes in the same frame). Beta behaves identically at boot. A warm run at 126 s on 4G still had the sweep running.

### 3.2 Causes, ranked

1. **The boot tier is 315 requests, 17.7 MB, 112 MB decoded, and the title waits on all of it or a 12 s cap.** `loadArt` (`art.ts:637-716`) fires everything in one `Promise.all`: 34 suit stills, 30 helmet stills and 20 pal stills for a hangar the player has not opened, 41 vortex frames, 27 legacy debris, 35 pickup frames, 14 Hyper Run files for a star-gated mode, 18 Debris Field ship parts, the Flight bank, and because the tutorial suit is AcorNut (`catalog.ts:116`, `engine.ts:1825`) his 16 frames at 512 px plus a 1024x768 atlas (3.2 MB, 20 MB decoded). At 4 Mbps that is at least 35 s of transfer, so the cap always fires on 4G and the rest decodes during the first run.
2. **The idle sweep runs during the first flight.** `engine.ts:1832` starts `prefetchArtBanks` the moment boot resolves; `art.ts:621-635` breathes 300 ms between banks but `many()` (`art.ts:225`) fetches a whole bank in one `Promise.all`, so a 36-frame pal lands as 36 back-to-back decodes. Nothing checks `world.screen`. 986 files, 53 MB, 288 MB decoded.
3. **Every sprite is measured on the main thread with a per-pixel loop, a `Math.hypot` per solid pixel and a comparator sort** (`measureSprite`, `art.ts:151-214`, called for all 1,300 sprites). Cold first-run profile: 25-30% of main-thread time (`measureSprite` 12.6%, the sort 6.4%, `getImageData` 5.9%). Per sprite at 4x CPU: 16-80 ms for a 256 px frame, 480-580 ms for a 1024 px atlas. The result is a pure function of the PNG. This cost is identical in the packaged app.
4. **Decode is unbounded and never deferred**: `new Image()` with `onload`, no `createImageBitmap`, no `decode()`, no worker, no concurrency cap; 329 requests open at once.
5. **The module graph is a 12-level dynamic-import waterfall**: 53 files, 1.9 MB, code ready at 5.0 s at 150 ms round trip, no `modulepreload`, no bundle. Production also pulls the 221 KB beta manifest because `campaign.ts:1` imports it unconditionally. Web only; local files collapse this to about 0.3 s.
6. **The resolution probe measures the loading storm and keeps the answer.** `noteFrameCost` (`engine.ts:1294-1307`) takes the median of the first 90 play frames and drops the render cap from DPR 3 to 2.5 once, for the session. On every measured run those frames were all over 33 ms because of 1-3, so every fresh install gets the pixel cut from a transient.
7. **Launch is a 0.9 s task at phone speed** (course build, procedural sky, the 1.8 MB zone scene, `flight.mp3`).
8. **Formats and sizes.** 30 PNGs with no alpha total 31 MB (zone scenes 2172x724, spill scenes, shop banners) and would be 5-10x smaller as JPEG/WebP. Pickups are drawn at 26-34 CSS px from 256 px sources. 1,497 of 1,608 images are 256 px squares.
9. **Not causes, checked.** The sim costs 21 ms per 1,200 steps; per-frame allocations are five filters; gradients and `shadowBlur` are confined to fog, vortex, race and spill; no `new Image` in draw or sim; the fixed step caps catch-up at 15 steps (0.3 ms), so there is no spiral of death. Music streams on demand and the 14.5 MB wide film is never fetched on a phone.

### 3.3 Fixes

Quick wins, about one day in total:

| # | Fix | Where | Effort | Effect |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| L1 | **Pause the sweep while `world.screen === "play"`** and for 2 s after launch; cap `many()` at 4 decodes in flight | `art.ts:225, 621-635`; a hook in the engine loop | 3 h | Removes every sweep hitch from the first flight (13 unthrottled, 100+ at phone speed) |
| L2 | **Trim the boot tier to what the tutorial and hub draw**: stills to hangar-open; vortex, pickup animations, non-zone-0 debris, Hyper Run and Debris Field parts to the sweep or mode-open; the Flight bank after the tutorial | `art.ts:637-716`, `engine.ts:1825` | 4 h | 315 → 77 requests, 17.7 → 6.3 MB, 112 → 47 MB decoded before the title; about 13 s of transfer on 4G instead of 35 s |
| L3 | **Export sprite metrics offline** (`docs/art/sprite-metrics.json` written by the art pipeline; `asSprite` reads it and measures only an unlisted file) | `art.ts:151-222`, `verify-art.py` or the exporters | 5 h | Removes 25-30% of cold first-run main-thread time and 1,300 transient canvases; the one fix that helps the packaged app as much as the web |
| L4 | **Bundle `standalone.js`** (esbuild, one file, same `js<VER>/` layout) or emit `modulepreload` for all 53 modules; drop the beta manifest from the production graph | `docs/index.html:2325`, the export script, `campaign.ts:1` | 2 h | Code ready at about 1.5 s instead of 5.0 s on 4G |
| L5 | **An honest loader**: count image loads against the boot list and show a bar that ends; with L2 the cap can drop to 6 s | `standalone.ts:113-162` | 1 h | No byte change; the wait reads as progress |
| L6 | Start the resolution probe only after no image has loaded for 2 s, and let it re-arm once | `engine.ts:1294-1307` | 1 h | A fresh install stops losing 30% of its pixels to a transient |

Expected together at phone speed: title at 8-9 s instead of 17.8 s, a first flight with no boot or sweep decodes under it, long-task time in the first minute from 31 s to about 4 s.

Structural, about a week, in order of return: pack banks into sheets like the premium suits already are (one decode per bank; requests from 986 to about 55), convert the 30 opaque PNGs and the transparent frames to WebP (web transfer 157 MB to about 70), ship pickups at 128 px, evict banks of un-equipped pals and suits when leaving the hangar (resident decoded from 397 MB to under 300), and decode off the main thread with `createImageBitmap` in a worker.

### 3.4 A load budget for the packaged app, and the test that holds it

With all art local the network goes away but every decode and every measure stays: the unthrottled run is the closest proxy, about 315 decodes before the title and 986 during the first flight. Resident decoded images after the sweep are about 400 MB and nothing ever evicts; if every asset is touched it is 867 MB. That fits under WKWebView's roughly 1-1.5 GB ceiling on a 4 GB phone, but WebKit purges decoded images under pressure and re-decodes them on the next draw, on the main thread, so on 2-3 GB devices the sweep turns memory pressure back into hitches.

| Tier | Budget | Today |
|---|---|---|
| Boot, before the title | 50 MB decoded, 80 files | 112 MB, 315 files |
| Sweep | 150 MB decoded, never while playing, 4 decodes in flight | 288 MB, 986 files, during play, 36 at a time |
| Per run, on demand (zone planets, debris, sky, scene) | 15 MB decoded, 8 files, one zone ahead | 14 MB, already done |
| Resident decoded images | 300 MB with eviction | 397 MB and growing with the catalog |
| Main thread, first 20 s of a run | 3 long tasks, no image loads outside the current and next zone | 25 hitches unthrottled, 73 of 73 frames at phone speed |

`illustrated-src/test-load-budget.mjs`, picked up by `run-tests.mjs` like every other test: a static half that derives the tier lists from `art.ts`, reads each file's dimensions from its header and asserts the table (seconds, no browser), and a Playwright half (about 40 s, heavy-exempt) that boots a fresh profile at CPU 4x, taps through to LAUNCH and asserts no image load outside the zone files during the first 20 s, at most 3 long tasks over 100 ms, and resident decoded bytes under 300 MB after the sweep.

### 3.5 Owner decisions

1. Boot tier contents (L2): the hangar's 82 stills would arrive over the first second after it opens on 4G; instant in the app.
2. Exported metrics (L3): keep runtime measuring as a fallback for a hand-dropped PNG (recommended).
3. Formats: the opaque PNGs are a pure win; lossy WebP for the 1,200 painted frames needs an eyeball pass on the masters.
4. A memory ceiling for the app (300 MB proposed) decides whether eviction is needed before submission or can follow.
5. Skip the intro film automatically while boot art is under 100%; drop the 14.5 MB wide film from the app bundle (it never reaches a phone).
6. SHIPPING.md §4 needs re-measuring after any of this; its numbers are off by 6x and are what the tiers were designed against.
